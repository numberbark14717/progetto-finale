The war of the worlds

The War of the Worlds is a science-fiction novel by H. G. Wells. It tells the story of a Martian invasion of Earth and how humans try to survive.

The Martians arrive

One day, strange metal cylinders fall from the sky. People think they are meteorites, but they are actually spaceships from Mars. When the first cylinder opens, a Martian comes out. It is not friendly: it immediately attacks humans with a heat ray, a weapon that burns everything.

The invasion spreads

More cylinders arrive, and the Martians build huge machines called Tripods. These machines walk on three legs and destroy cities easily. The army tries to stop them, but the Martians are too strong and have deadly weapons. People run away, and society collapses.

How it ends

Humans cannot defeat the Martians. But the Martians are not adapted to Earth: they die because of bacteria and germs that are harmless to humans. In the end, Earth is saved not by weapons, but by nature, showing to humans that their more insignificant than they think

I suoni di Marte

Tecnología española para la atmósfera

España tiene un papel importante en las misiones a Marte, sobre todo gracias al Centro de Astrobiología y a la colaboración con la ESA.
Sus aportes principales son las estaciones meteorológicas REMS y MEDA en los rovers de la NASA, que miden el clima marciano, y la participación en el experimento MOXIE, que produce oxígeno a partir del dióxido de carbono de Marte.
España también trabaja en componentes para el rover europeo Rosalind Franklin.
